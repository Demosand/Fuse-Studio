# Game loops, progression and retention

Start with **player action -> immediate feedback -> reward -> meaningful progression -> new decision**. Good retention comes from enjoyable play and clear goals, not manipulative friction. New-player fun must exist before a daily reward or timed offer.

## Core loop worksheet

- Fantasy: what does player *do* in one verb? Why is it satisfying?
- Micro loop (seconds): input, risk/tension, sensory response, mastery.
- Session loop (minutes): objective, reward, choice, escalating challenge.
- Meta loop (days): collection, skill, build, unlock or social reason to return.
- Signature twist: one meaningful mechanic that differentiates experience, not just a skin.

## Economy

Map **sources** (quest, sale, drop, login, Robux) against **sinks** (items, upgrade, fee, prestige). Write expected rewards/hour by player stage and target cost; watch runaway inflation and grind walls. Track edge cases: replayable infinite rewards, resale duplication, client-owned prices, negative costs, refunded purchases.

## RNG and disclosure

Clear in-world rarity and reveal timing matter for fairness and excitement. If randomness is tied to paid consideration, verify current Roblox paid-random-item policies, region/age restrictions, disclosures, and permissions; never fake probability. Use transparent player-facing copy.

## Onboarding

Give a meaningful first interaction quickly; explain through play, not multiple screens. Add skip/replay for tutorials, readable quest feedback, no forced purchase. Give a visible next goal after every meaningful reward.

## Live operations

Prefer voluntary, transparent events with attainable rewards. Use analytics responsibly: onboarding completion, meaningful retention, voluntary return, source/sink balance and churn reasons; optimize enjoyment, not excessive compulsion.
