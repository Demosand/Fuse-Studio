# Cinematic VFX art direction

## A VFX is an event with meaning
Design in five layers: anticipation (read action), focal impact (cause/effect), directional shape (trajectory), secondary debris/sparks (material), dissipate/smoke (cooldown). Choose silhouette and timing before spawn counts. Match material: electricity arcs, fire blooms, sword slash ribbon, ice crystalline breakup, magic dust. Favor 2–3 complementary colors, luminance contrast and hierarchy.

## Storyboard with milliseconds
Outline activation, travel, contact, peak, residual and fade. Verify event timing tied to gameplay hit window; don't mask the actual signal. Keep cosmetic trails client-side and damage server-side. Short-lived lighting pulses when useful, not global atmosphere changes on every hit.

## Delivery
Specify particle/emitter lifetime, speed/spread, beams/trails, attachment placement, textures and any missing asset IDs; do not invent Roblox marketplace assets. Document mobile quality, low-photosensitivity mode, object pooling, automatic cleanup, visual acceptance reference.
