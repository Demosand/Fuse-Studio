# Art-directed lighting, atmospheres and night scenes

## Lighting is composition
Use a key light to direct eyes, a controlled fill to preserve silhouettes, color contrast for depth and background shapes to separate planes. Pick a mood board and time-of-day; tune exposure, atmosphere and surface materials intentionally. A believable night scene still needs navigable ground and readable players.

## Roblox practicals
Be careful with `Lighting`, `Atmosphere`, `ColorCorrectionEffect`, `BloomEffect`, `DepthOfFieldEffect`, `SunRaysEffect` and shadows—some may be expensive or harmful to readability. Local lights should have limited range and reason to exist. Device quality tiers may disable expensive postprocessing.

## Validation
Capture same scene low/medium/high quality and mobile. Check flickering, excessive bloom, night vision of player outlines, over-dark menus. Avoid server-wide Environment changes for momentary personal abilities unless intended for all.
