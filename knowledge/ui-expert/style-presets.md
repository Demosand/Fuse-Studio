# Consistent style presets

- Stud / Chunky Simulator: beveled 3D square stud buttons, vibrant gradients, sticker icons, heavily outlined extruded type, bouncy motion, dark-glass windows. Great for tycoon and pet simulators. Use real kit in vendor/stud-ui.
- Premium Simulator: vivid cards, rarity tags and purchase CTAs, smooth modern gradients, approachable soft edges. For RNG/progression games.
- Modern Minimal: crisp information hierarchy, whitespace, restrained colors and typography, calm transitions. For settings or complex inventory.
- Glass/Aurora: translucent surfaces, controlled blur, glowing colored accents and crisp opaque text. For social and futuristic games.
- Sci-Fi/Cyberpunk: technical lines, limited neon accents, dense but readable data labels and terminal motifs. For cyberpunk games.
- Tactical HUD: functional ammo/health priority, reticle-safe layout and minimal distraction while aiming. For FPS, PvP and survival.
- Retro Pixel/Arcade: pixel-aligned frames, stepped edges, limited palette, quick rhythmic animation. For arcade games.
- Cartoon/Playful: expressive iconography, bold outlines, rounded/cut shapes, squash-and-stretch. For party or obby games.
- Fantasy/RPG: wood, stone or metallic decorative frames, emblematic headers and readable stats. For roleplay adventures.

For ALL styles: touch-safe sizes, device-safe insets, controller focus, readability, localization, reduced motion, cleanup of tweens/connections, server-side purchase validation and mobile performance. Never invent asset IDs. Persist a chosen style throughout the project.

For Stud use knowledge/ui-expert/stud-ui-design and original MIT source in vendor/stud-ui. For other styles consult existing knowledge/ui-expert/visual-direction, tokens-theme-system, responsive-layout-engine and motion-orchestration; do not claim other prebuilt kits exist unless they really do.
